What is PassLok?

PassLok provides end-to-end encryption for text, files, and real-time chat sessions. It operates on a "Lock and Key" principle: you encrypt using a recipient's public Lock, and they decrypt using their private Key. Because there is no central server, no private data ever leaves your device. It is portable across computers, phones, and tablets via copy-paste. Note: PassLok is experimental; use caution for critical secrets. Alternatives include SeeOnce (Read-once), URSA (shared Keys), and the FusionKey browser extension.

Invite others & Display your Lock

Before communicating, others must generate a Lock from their own Key and share it with you.

1. QR Invitation: Click Invite with an empty main box to display a QR code containing your Lock and an app link.

2. Email Invitation: Type a message and click Invite to open an email draft containing your Lock and instructions.

3. Manual Display: Click myLock to display your Lock in ezLok, Word, or base64 format for manual sharing.

To authenticate your identity, you can post a video of yourself reading the first 15 characters of your Lock aloud.

Making a Strong Key & Guest Mode

Your Key should be memorable as it is never stored and is erased from memory after five minutes of inactivity. Use caPiTals, numb3rs, and $ymbol$; avoid common words. As you type, a color-coded strength score and a "Hashili" mnemonic word appear to help you verify the Key without revealing the text. If PassLok runs slowly, it is likely because your Key is weak and requires extra computations.

Guest Mode: Click Cancel at the Key prompt to access the directory and encrypt items without a personal Key. You cannot decrypt items meant for your Lock in this mode.

Managing the Local Directory

1. Add from Main: Paste a Lock into the Main box; you will be prompted for a name if recognized.

2. Add from Edit: Click Edit next to the directory, paste the Lock or shared Key, and click Save.

3. Rename/Delete: Select an item in the Edit dialog, click Rename, and enter a new name or "Delete" to remove it.

4. Lists: You can save multiple items as a List (bracketed by double dashes) to encrypt for several people at once.

Encryption: Anonymous Mode (PL**msa)

Standard mode where the sender's identity is hidden.

1. Select Anonymous at the bottom of the Main tab.

2. Select recipients in the directory or paste their Locks in the Edit dialog.

3. Write your message (use Rich for files) and click Encrypt.

Check Include my Lock in Options if you want the recipient to receive your public Lock with the message.

Encryption: Signed (PL**mss) and Read-once (PL**mso)

Signed Mode: Encrypts using your Lock so the recipient can verify you as the sender. Decryption requires the recipient to have your Lock stored.

Read-once Mode: Messages become unreadable after one decryption. 1. Select Read-once. 2. Select stored recipients. 3. Click Encrypt. If a conversation loses sync, select the contact in Edit and click Reset to re-initiate.

Shared Keys, Pads, and Human Mode

Shared Keys: Agree on a phrase with your recipient and save it in Edit. Encrypt normally.

Pad Mode (PL**msp): Paste a shared text 5x longer than your message into the Edit box. Click Encrypt and enter a starting numerical position when prompted.

Human Mode (PL**msh): Uses keys made of four text strings separated by tildes (~). This mode is designed for manual encryption/decryption with paper and pencil.

Loading and Retrieving Files

1. Click the "Load file" icon on the Rich toolbar.

2. Select your file; it appears as a link. You can add multiple files or images.

3. To retrieve, click the Save button below the Main box after decryption.

Enable File output in Options to handle large files faster as .plk or .txt files. Use File Drop Mode in Options for drag-and-drop encryption.

Concealing Data (Steganography)

Text Hiding: Click Text hide to turn output into cover text. Modes: Invisible, Letters (look-alike symbols), Words, Spaces, or Sentences. To reveal, paste text and click Text hide.

Image Hiding: 1. Click Image hide and load a photo. 2. Enter an optional password. 3. Click PNG hide or JPG hide. 4. Save the result. To reveal, load the image and click Reveal. Warning: iOS currently cannot reveal hidden image data.

Secure Real-Time Chat

1. Select participants and click Chat.

2. Choose Text, Audio, Video, or Jitsi and click OK.

3. Send the generated invitation (PL**chat) to others.

4. To join, paste an invitation and click Chat.

Connections are direct (except Jitsi). Verify identities in chat by sending garbage text for others to Seal; if you can Unseal it with their stored Lock, they are authenticated.

Advanced: Splitting, Seals, and Hidden Messages

Split (PL**p^^^): Click Split to break an item into parts; enter the total parts and the minimum "quorum" needed to rejoin.

Seals (PL**sld): Click Seal to sign a text. This is NOT encryption; others verify it via Unseal to confirm the sender.

Hidden Message: Enable in Options. During standard encryption, you'll be prompted for a second undetectable message and key.

Backups, User Change, and Checksums

Backups: Click Options only to backup your token, or Whole Directory to export your contacts (PL**dir). Change Info: Use Name, Key, or Email/Token buttons in Options to update your profile.

Checksums: Verify code integrity by comparing the SHA256 checksum of this page against values at passlok.weebly.com. Watch the author's video to confirm the values audibly.

Privacy Policy and Warrant Canary
